# AI Video Studio

> A growing collection of AI-assisted videos across education, storytelling and visual communication.

**Tech:** HTML, CSS, HTML5 Video
**Tools:** GitHub Pages, Canva
**AI Tools:** ChatGPT, Canva AI Voice
**Live:** https://hemasri-kalaiselvan.github.io/ai-videos/

## About

AI Video Studio explores how generative-AI tools can support the creation of short, engaging visual content. The website is organized into galleries so that educational videos, storytelling, explainers and other future categories can live together in one project.

The first available gallery is **Educational Videos**. Its first sample explains why Earth experiences day and night in ten seconds.

### Why Do We Have Day and Night?

- **Subject:** Science
- **Audience:** School students
- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Format:** Vertical, 9:16
- **Learning point:** Earth’s rotation causes day and night.

## Script

> Why do we have day and night? Earth rotates on its axis. The side facing the Sun has day, while the other side has night. One rotation takes twenty-four hours.

## Creation Process

1. Selected a simple school-level science concept.
2. Prepared a concise and age-appropriate script.
3. Checked the explanation for scientific accuracy.
4. Created and arranged three supporting visuals.
5. Added AI-generated narration.
6. Synchronized captions with the narration and visuals.
7. Reviewed the exported video for readability, timing and mobile display.

## Human Oversight

This project is AI-assisted, not fully automated. Topic selection, fact-checking, visual sequencing, caption correction and final quality review were completed with human oversight.

## Future Direction
